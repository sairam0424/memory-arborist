# Code of conduct

Be kind and be specific. This project is a place to fix a tool, and everyone taking part, in issues and pull requests, is expected to:

- treat others with respect, and disagree about the work, not about the person;
- accept review of their own work as readily as they give it;
- keep other people's private information out of the project: no notes, transcripts, keys or personal details in anything you post.

Harassment, insults, discrimination and publishing someone's private information are not welcome. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this.

To report a conduct problem, use the private route described in `SECURITY.md` and say it is about conduct (in the issue form's channel box, type the word `conduct`). Reports are read by one person and treated as private.
